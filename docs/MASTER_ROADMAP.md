# PolisLab master development roadmap

**25 November 2026 · post-v0.2.0 baseline**

Build 09C is closed. Production v0.2.0 is the accepted release at `af608a78d1db0caa7a2ca8340a535dcdadf4ca0b`. It presents the Australian grocery evidence atlas, investigations, mechanism profiles, qualitative comparison and research gaps. The published v0.1.1 corpus and its licence remain the immutable evidence baseline. The [research invariants](RESEARCH_INVARIANTS.md) govern the current application.

The programme aims to make PolisLab an interactive, game-like political-economy laboratory where people can investigate how institutions and market behaviour interact from individual sites to national and international systems. Each expansion depends on evidence and verification at its own scale. Production v0.2.1 supports evidence exploration and qualitative comparison. The local v0.3 candidate adds a guided, hypothetical Analytical Sandbox with versioned replays. Public spatial simulation is a future milestone.

## Governance and sequencing

1. **This cycle** delivers rendered UX directions and a research charter for separate owner decisions. Neither depends on the other's approval. Keep unpublished working material in local private storage. Use the [development ledger](DEVELOPMENT_LEDGER.md) and AgentHarness project journal for continuity.
2. **After design approval** test a single end-to-end investigation first. Advance the full library, comparisons and casebook only after that slice demonstrates source fidelity and usability. Retain legacy URLs as stable entry points.
3. **After charter approval** acquire and document a bounded cohort, adjudicate data coverage, and preregister model assumptions and falsifiers before fitting. If data fail the gate, deliver a transparent feasibility result or revise the design for separate approval.
4. **At every promotion** replay the exact applicable receipts, run uncovered checks, and record distinct results for data integrity, engineering, scientific validity and release authority. Registered AgentHarness release gates do not qualify a new model or a prototype. Use existing capabilities and add scope only when justified by observed needs.
5. **Always** preserve the accepted release and the pinned research corpus. Keep private research, credentials, receipts and machine configuration out of public history. Additional expenditure remains AUD 0.00.

The research programme continues across H2-E documentary evidence, H2-T theory and H2-C reproducible computation. H2-E remains a two-case descriptive programme with estimation held. Spatial work proceeds through staged method, verification and disclosure reviews. Academic novelty and independent external scientific review remain open.
